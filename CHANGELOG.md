# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release.

- A block for the association's website, Opening hours, that the board puts on
  a page from the page editor and fills in with a heading, when it opens and
  closes, and a note. The hours show only when both times are given and are
  times, written `07.00` on a Swedish page and `07:00` on an English one. A
  block with no hours and no note renders nothing.
- It asks for `site:blocks` alone and declares no personal data.
- Swedish and English.
